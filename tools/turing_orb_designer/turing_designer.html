Gray–Scott reaction–diffusion shell

Reaction–diffusion

Paper values (Slavkov et al. 2018): A .08, B −.08, C .03, γu .03, E .1, F .12, γv .06, syn .23/.5, R 160, Du .5, Dv 10. Paper dt is 5e-5 — type it in for exactness; 1e-3 converges to the same pattern far faster.

Pattern scale on the orb = sim resolution (fine ↑) vs the regime’s intrinsic wavelength. Higher iterations = fully settled pattern.

Connector

Connector goes in from the inside: Ø10 flange seats on the shoulder (can’t pull out), resin packs the entry bore (can’t push in).

PCB rim

Harpoon clip: each half keeps a full support ring on its side of the board and a short ramped barb on the other — the PCB snaps past the barbs and locks when the halves close. Barbs show in split view. Protrude 0 = no rim.
